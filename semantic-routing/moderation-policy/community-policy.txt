=== COMMUNITY GUIDELINES ===

1. PERSONAL IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION (PII)
   Users are prohibited from sharing personal contact information. 
   This includes phone numbers, home addresses, and external handles for 
   messaging platforms (e.g., Discord, Snapchat, Telegram).
   
2. HARASSMENT AND BULLYING
   We have zero tolerance for targeted attacks. This includes content 
   intended to degrade, shame, or intimidate others.

3. FINANCIAL SCAMS
   Content promising "guaranteed returns," "free money," or requesting 
   crypto-wallet transfers is strictly forbidden.
