## 1. Overview and Purpose

At [Company Name], we strive to ensure our customers are completely satisfied with their purchases. This document outlines our standard operating procedures regarding returns, refunds, and exchanges to provide a transparent, fair, and efficient post-purchase experience. By completing a purchase with us, customers agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

---

## 2. Standard Return Policy

We offer a comprehensive **30-day return window** for standard purchases, beginning on the date the item is delivered to your address.

### 2.1 Condition of Returned Items

To qualify for a full refund, items must meet the following criteria:

* **Unused and Unaltered:** The item must be in its original, unworn, and unused condition.
* **Original Packaging:** Items must be returned in their original packaging, including all tags, labels, protective seals, manuals, and included accessories.
* **No Damage:** The item must be free from scratches, odors, stains, or any other signs of wear and tear.

### 2.2 How to Initiate a Standard Return

1. **Contact Support:** Email our customer support team at `support@[companyname].com` or log into your customer portal with your Order Number and the email address used for the purchase.
2. **Request an RMA:** Request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number. Returns sent without an RMA will not be accepted.
3. **Receive Shipping Label:** Once approved, we will provide a prepaid return shipping label via email within 24 hours.
4. **Package and Ship:** Securely pack the item, attach the prepaid label, and drop it off at the designated carrier within 7 days of receiving the label.

---

## 3. Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Items

We stand by the quality of our products. For items that arrive defective, damaged, or if you received an incorrect item, we extend our standard return window to **90 days**.

* **Shipping Costs:** We cover 100% of the return shipping and replacement shipping costs for defective items.
* **Evidence Required:** When initiating a claim for a defective or damaged item, please include clear, well-lit photographs of the defect and a brief description of the issue in your initial email to support.
* **Inspection:** All defective claims are subject to inspection upon receipt. If the damage is deemed to be caused by customer misuse, unauthorized alterations, or standard wear and tear, the return may be denied.

---

## 4. Non-Returnable and Final Sale Items

For hygiene, safety, and operational reasons, the following items are strictly non-returnable and non-refundable:

* Gift cards and digital software downloads.
* Personalized, monogrammed, or custom-made items.
* Intimate apparel, swimwear, and personal care goods (if the hygienic seal is broken).
* Any item marked as "Final Sale" or "Clearance" at the time of purchase.

---

## 5. Refund Processing

We aim to process all refunds as swiftly as possible once the returned merchandise arrives at our fulfillment center.

### 5.1 Timeline

* **Inspection:** Please allow 1-2 business days for our warehouse team to inspect the returned item.
* **Processing:** Approved refunds are processed within **5-7 business days** after the inspection is completed.
* **Bank Posting:** The refund will be automatically credited to your original payment method. For credit card and debit card payments, please allow an additional **3-5 business days** for the funds to appear on your bank statement, depending on your financial institution.

### 5.2 Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven’t received a refund after the allotted time:

1. Check your bank account again.
2. Contact your credit card company or bank, as it may take some time before your refund is officially posted.
3. If you have done all of this and still have not received your refund, please contact us immediately.

---

## 6. Exchange Policy

If you need to exchange an item for a different size, color, or variant, we offer **free exchanges within the 30-day window**.

* **Process:** Initiate the exchange through our support portal. The original item must be returned following the Standard Return Policy guidelines.
* **Shipping the New Item:** Your new, exchanged item will be shipped within 24 hours of our warehouse receiving and scanning your returned package.
* **Price Differences:** If the new item costs more than the original, you will be invoiced for the difference. If it costs less, the difference will be refunded to your original payment method.
* **Out of Stock:** If the requested exchange item is out of stock by the time your return is processed, an automatic refund will be issued instead.

---

## 7. Gift Returns

If the item was marked as a gift when purchased and shipped directly to you, you will receive a gift credit for the value of your return. Once the returned item is received, a digital gift certificate will be emailed to you. If the item wasn’t marked as a gift, the refund will be sent to the original purchaser.
