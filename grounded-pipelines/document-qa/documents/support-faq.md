## 1. Account Management & Security

### **Q: How do I reset my password?**

If you have forgotten your password or suspect unauthorized access:

1. Click the **"Forgot Password?"** link on the main login screen.
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. Check your inbox for a secure reset link (valid for **24 hours**).
4. If you do not see the email within 5 minutes, please check your Spam/Junk folder or ensure `noreply@[domain].com` is whitelisted.

> **Note:** For security purposes, you cannot reuse your last 3 passwords.

### **Q: How do I change my registered email address?**

To update your primary contact email:

1. Navigate to **Account Settings > Profile > Email Preferences**.
2. Enter your new email address and current password for verification.
3. A confirmation link will be sent to the **new** email address. You must click this link to finalize the change.
4. Once verified, all future notifications and login prompts will use the new address.

### **Q: How do I enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?**

We highly recommend enabling 2FA for added security. Go to **Account Settings > Security**. We support authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy) and SMS verification. Once enabled, you will need to provide a one-time code upon every login.

### **Q: How do I delete my account?**

Account deletion is a permanent action. To initiate this:

1. Submit a formal request via the "Privacy" tab in your settings or contact support directly.
2. Our team will process the request within **30 days** in compliance with GDPR/CCPA regulations.
3. **Warning:** This action is **irreversible**. All data, configurations, and history will be permanently wiped from our servers. We strongly suggest exporting your data before proceeding.

## 2. Billing, Subscriptions, & Payments

### **Q: What payment methods do you accept?**

We offer flexible payment options based on your plan type:

* **Individual/Team Plans:** Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and PayPal.
* **Enterprise Plans:** All the above, plus ACH transfers, SWIFT wire transfers, and Purchase Orders (PO) upon credit approval.

### **Q: How do I update my payment method?**

You can manage your wallet at any time:

1. Go to **Account Settings > Billing > Payment Methods**.
2. Select "Add New Method" to input new card details.
3. Set the new card as "Default" to ensure future renewals are charged correctly.
4. You may delete old payment methods only after a new default has been established.

### **Q: Where can I find my invoices?**

For expense reporting and tax purposes:

1. Navigate to **Account Settings > Billing > Invoice History**.
2. You will see a chronological list of all charges.
3. Click the **download icon** next to any transaction to retrieve a PDF copy containing our tax ID and your billing address.

### **Q: What happens if my payment fails?**

If a renewal charge is declined, our system will attempt to retry the charge 3 times over a 7-day grace period. You will retain access to your account during this time. If payment is not secured after the grace period, the account will be temporarily suspended until the balance is resolved.

## 3. Technical Support & Troubleshooting

### **Q: How do I contact support?**

We offer multiple channels depending on your urgency:

* **Email Support:** Send inquiries to `support@example.com`. A ticket number will be automatically generated and emailed to you.
* **Live Chat:** Access the chat widget in the bottom-right corner of the dashboard.
* *Availability:* Monday – Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST.


* **Help Center:** Access our self-service tutorials and community forums 24/7 via the "Help" tab.

### **Q: What is your standard response time (SLA)?**

* **Standard Users:** We aim to respond to all inquiries within **24 business hours**.
* **Priority/Enterprise:** Guaranteed response within **4 hours** during business days, with weekend coverage for critical system outages.

### **Q: How do I report a bug or system error?**

To help us resolve technical issues faster, please include the following when contacting support:

1. **Screenshots** or screen recordings of the error.
2. **Steps to Reproduce:** A brief list of actions you took before the error occurred.
3. **Log Files:** If using our desktop app, attach the log files found in the `Help > Export Logs` menu.
